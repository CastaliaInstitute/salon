A STORMY EVENING

Salon transcript

An imagined exchange among the guests—not dialogue from the film or a historical transcript.

Lord Byron

This rain has made a prison of the villa.

Mary Shelley

Then let us make something worth staying in for.

Percy Bysshe Shelley

A thought can travel farther than any of us tonight.

Claire Clairmont

Only if someone is brave enough to begin it.

John Polidori

Very well. Let us give the darkness a voice.
